Third-party software

The runtime SBOMs identify the exact dependency versions in each artifact.
The project's MIT license is included separately as project/LICENSE in the JARs
and LICENSE in the release bundle. It does not replace dependency licenses.

Apache-2.0 software

The complete license is included in Apache-2.0.txt.

Both artifacts include:
  io.nats:jnats — https://github.com/nats-io/nats.java
  org.jspecify:jspecify — https://github.com/jspecify/jspecify

The consumer additionally includes:
  com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-core
  com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-databind
  com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-annotations
    https://github.com/FasterXML
  com.zaxxer:HikariCP — https://github.com/brettwooldridge/HikariCP

Packaging preserves upstream LICENSE and NOTICE resources, combining resources
that share the same path. Jackson's named FastDoubleParser and Schubfach license
resources and PostgreSQL's named third-party license resources remain in META-INF.

Other dependency notices

Bouncy Castle's complete MIT copyright, permission and disclaimer text remains
in org.bouncycastle.LICENSE (relocated in the provider artifact). SLF4J's MIT
license, PostgreSQL's license and its embedded dependency licenses remain in the
consumer's META-INF resources. The consumer also includes Checker Framework
annotations; their complete MIT notice is included in Checker-qual-MIT.txt.

The provider relocates the JNATS and Bouncy Castle packages under
io.github.gbeaule.keycloaknats.internal. These are packaging changes; the
dependencies retain their upstream licenses.
